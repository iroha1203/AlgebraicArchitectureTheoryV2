## AAT and SFT

AAT and SFT are entirely new theories that I am building.

### AAT (Algebraic Architecture Theory)

AAT is an attempt to speak about software architecture in a unified way, through algebraic geometry.
It extracts the constituents of source code as an intermediate representation called atoms, and writes specifications and constraints as algebraic equations acting on those atoms — and with that, the geometry of a software architecture becomes something we can talk about.
Software architecture has long been discussed through rules of thumb and one-off methodologies. By raising the level of abstraction, AAT aims for a Rising Sea in which those problems dissolve into a unified approach.

### SFT (Software Field Theory)

If AAT gives software its geometry, SFT is the dynamics that lives on that geometry.
Along the time axis of development, it aims to make computable how software evolves and where it is heading.
Going further, by treating the whole development organization as a participant in the field, it aims to become a dynamical system of the organization itself.

## The name and the subdomain

The subdomain of this blog is "ideal".
And the blog's name is "Spec Z".
If you know some algebraic geometry, this may ring a bell: they come from ideals in algebra, and from Spec Z.

### Spec Z

In mathematics, Z is the ring of integers, and its spectrum is the space made of the zero ideal (0) and the prime ideals (p), one for each prime number p.
Spec Z has:

- (0) as its generic point
- closed points (2), (3), (5), (7), ... appearing prime by prime

A discrete world of numbers shows up as a single geometric space —
that is what Spec Z means.

Software, mathematics, philosophy, AI: these "discrete" subjects, I want to scatter them not as unrelated topics, but as points that together make up one space. That is the wish I put into the name.

### Ideal

In everyday language, "ideal" means an aspiration, a guiding principle.
At the same time, in mathematics, it names the ideals living inside a ring.
And the points of Spec Z are exactly the prime ideals of Z — (0) and the primes.

The subdomain "ideal" carries this image: each post is a point holding an ideal — in both senses of the word — scattered across Spec Z.

### As the base

In mathematics, Spec Z is the base space: in algebraic geometry, every scheme stands over it.
In the same way, I want this blog to be the base of the thought beneath AAT and SFT — the place where its foundations are written down: the ways of thinking, the principles, the ideals.

I want this blog to be like that: irregular, but something that keeps going for a long, long time.
